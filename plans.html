How Our Outreach Model Works

Attempt-based model: You pay a flat monthly rate for a set number of outbound contact attempts. An attempt is one outbound contact action through an approved channel (call, SMS, email, or DM) to a specific lead. This model gives you predictable costs with transparent reporting.

Performance-based booking: Want to pay only for results? Our performance tier charges per booked appointment instead of per attempt. A booked appointment = confirmed date/time on calendar meeting minimum qualification rules. No-shows, cancellations, duplicates, and unqualified leads do not count.

Definitions & Policies

Attempt+

An attempt is one outbound contact action through an approved channel (call, SMS, email, or DM) to a specific lead. Each unique contact to a unique lead counts as one attempt. Follow-up attempts to the same lead are counted separately.

Lead+

A lead is a potential customer identified through sourcing, inbound interest, or referral who has not yet booked an appointment. Leads are tracked in your CRM pipeline.

Appointment+

A confirmed booking on your calendar with a specific date, time, and service. The lead has agreed to attend and meets minimum qualification criteria.

Business Hours+

Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time. Can be customized during onboarding.

Ad Spend+

Ad spend is paid separately by the client. Boss Booker manages campaign setup, targeting, optimization, and reporting. You maintain direct control of your ad budget through the advertising platform (Google Ads, Meta, etc.).

Setup Fees+

One-time fees charged at the start of service for initial platform configuration, account creation, and onboarding. Setup fees are non-refundable and due before work begins.

Revisions+

Each tier includes a set number of revision rounds: Lite includes 1 round, Standard includes 2 rounds, Plus includes 3 rounds. A revision round covers one set of consolidated feedback. Extra revision rounds are $25 each.

Business Cards+

Printing included. Shipping billed separately. Pickup available. Template cards include 1 revision round. Custom cards include 2 revision rounds. Extra revision rounds: $25 each. Rush / same-day: +$35.

Performance-Based Booking+

Booked appointment = confirmed date/time on calendar meeting minimum qualification rules. No-shows, cancellations, duplicates, unqualified leads do not count. Pricing is custom and agreed upon during onboarding based on your industry, service value, and volume requirements.

GBP + Local Presence+

Google Business Profile optimization, local presence setup, citations basics, review workflow guidance, and basic on-page/local signals (where applicable). Full SEO (ongoing content + deeper optimization) is available by custom scope.
